# Green Zebra Grocery: Expansion Strategy & Financial Valuation

## Overview
This repository contains a strategic corporate finance case study evaluating the expansion options for Green Zebra Grocery, a Portland-based healthy convenience store startup. The analysis compares two distinct growth strategies: a conservative 1-store local expansion in Portland versus an aggressive 3-store regional expansion into Seattle backed by Venture Capital.

## Key Areas of Analysis
* **Financial Forecasting:** Built 5-year projections for Sales, COGS, EBITDA, and Net Income to determine cash burn and liquidity needs.
* **Investment Appraisal:** Calculated Net Present Value (NPV), Internal Rate of Return (IRR), and Profitability Index (PI) for both expansion options.
* **Business Valuation:** Conducted an Enterprise Value (EV) and Equity Valuation using the Multiples Approach (EV/Revenue and EV/EBITDA).
* **Strategic Risk Assessment:** Analyzed the implications of Venture Capital funding, ownership dilution, and the impact of economies of scale vs. operational complexity.

## Key Findings & Strategic Recommendation
1. **Financial Trade-offs:** The 3-store Seattle expansion yields a significantly higher absolute **NPV ($3.71M vs. $1.74M)**, while the 1-store Portland expansion offers a much higher **IRR (40.53% vs. 25.31%)** due to lower initial capital outlay.
2. **Valuation Impact:** The aggressive Seattle expansion scales the projected Enterprise Value to nearly $59M by 2025, aligning closely with a proposed $12M VC funding round.
3. **Recommendation:** We recommended the **Seattle (3+ Stores) Expansion**. While carrying higher operational risk, it maximizes long-term enterprise value, captures crucial economies of scale, and positions the brand for future high-growth fundraising rounds on the West Coast.
